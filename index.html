TRUST BOUNDARY

Transparent rules ≠ real-world quality.

Exact strings, bounded lengths, JSON keys and evidence IDs are structural tests only. They do not verify that evidence is genuine, that statements are true, or that a model is safe. No private data, client work, exchange actions, external AI calls or credentials are present.
